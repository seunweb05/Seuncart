# SEUNCART starter catalog photo notes

The 40-product starter catalog uses real photographs of the named products from Wikimedia Commons where available. Product-photo URLs use Wikimedia Commons' `Special:Redirect/file/...` endpoint so the browser loads the original media.

Some images are licensed under Creative Commons and require attribution/share-alike conditions. Before using the starter photos in a commercial production store, review each file's license and keep the required attribution. The admin dashboard is designed so you can replace any starter image with your own product photos through Cloudinary.

The iPhone 15 Pro starter photo is sourced from The Apple Wiki's archived official-product image because an exact Commons photograph was not found in the research pass. Treat that image as a demo reference and replace it with a photo you own or have permission to use before commercial launch.

For the production store, use the actual photos of the exact stock unit/variant you sell. Do not imply that a starter photo is a photo of the exact unit in your inventory.

Reference pages researched for the starter catalog include Wikimedia Commons files for Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra, Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra, Samsung Galaxy A55 5G, Google Pixel 8 Pro, OnePlus 13, Xiaomi 13, Huawei P60 Pro, MacBook Air, Microsoft Surface Laptop 7, HP Spectre x360, Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon, ASUS ROG Zephyrus G14, iPad Pro, iPad Air, AirPods Pro, AirPods Max, Samsung Galaxy Buds/Buds2, Samsung Galaxy Watch, Apple Watch, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X, Nintendo Switch OLED, DualSense, Logitech MX Master 3S and JBL Charge 4.
